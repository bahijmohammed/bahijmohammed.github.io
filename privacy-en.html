Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 · Version 3

This Privacy Policy explains how the Fixor mobile application ("Fixor"), published by VeDoxe ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: the business information you enter stays on your device and is not sent to us; Fixor displays advertisements from Google AdMob, and Google may process certain device and advertising data for that purpose.

1. Who we are

  • Publisher: VeDoxe
  • Location: Khemisset, Morocco.
  • Privacy contact: support@vedoxe.com

2. Information stored on your device

When you use Fixor, the information you choose to enter is stored on your device, in the App’s local database. It can include:

  • stores (names and an optional picture);
  • customers: names, optional phone numbers and notes;
  • suppliers: names, optional phone numbers and notes;
  • products, inventory, phone units and optional IMEI numbers;
  • sales, repairs, purchases, exchanges and trade-ins;
  • expenses, debts, payments and financial records;
  • loyalty programs, cards, stamps and rewards (including optional pictures);
  • the App’s settings (language, currency, theme).

3. Where this information is kept

This information is stored only in the App’s private storage on your device. It is not sent to our servers or to any cloud service, and we do not have access to it.

Fixor disables Android’s automatic cloud backup for its data, so the App’s database is not copied to cloud backups by Android.

4. When information leaves your device

Information leaves your device only when you decide so, for example when you share or save a backup file, an Excel export, a PDF report or a loyalty card image through Android’s sharing options. The file then goes to the app or place you choose, and its handling is subject to that service’s own terms and privacy policy.

When you import an Excel file or choose a picture, Fixor reads only the file you select.

5. No account, no cloud, no analytics

Fixor does not require or offer any account, registration or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address or password.

Fixor does not use Firebase Authentication, cloud synchronization or cloud storage for business records, and it does not include analytics or crash-reporting services.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Fixor displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To deliver advertisements, Google may collect and process, for example:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived;
  • device and app information (for example model, operating system, language and app version);
  • information about the ads shown and your interactions with them, and diagnostic information.

This information is used to deliver, measure and limit advertisements, prevent fraud and abuse and, only where you have agreed, personalize advertisements. On supported devices, the advertising SDK may also use Android’s privacy-preserving advertising features (such as Topics and attribution).

Fixor does not give advertisers your business information (customers, suppliers, products, transactions or records).

Google processes this information as described in its own policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Advertising consent (Google UMP)

Fixor uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and record consent for advertising where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). Advertisements are requested only after this process; without consent to personalization, advertisements are not personalized.

Where available, you can review or change your choices in Fixor under Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and limit ad personalization in your Android settings.

8. Permissions

Fixor uses internet and network-state access only for advertising and consent, and the advertising ID permission for advertising. Pictures and Excel files are chosen through Android’s system pickers. Fixor does not request access to your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

9. Third-party service providers

Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited, as provider of Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform: advertising and consent management. The other technical components included in Fixor work on your device and do not send your information to us.

10. Retention and deletion

Your information stays on your device until you delete it in the App (for example by deleting records or a store), clear the App’s data, or uninstall the App. Backup or export files you saved elsewhere are not deleted by the App and remain under your control.

Information processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google’s policies.

11. Backups

Because your information is not stored online, we cannot restore it if it is lost. You are responsible for making regular backups with the App’s backup or export features and for keeping these files secure.

12. Security

Fixor’s data is kept in the App’s private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Fixor does not encrypt its database or its backup and export files with an additional password. Protect your device with a screen lock, and be careful where you send or store backup and export files, because they may contain personal information.

13. Your customers’ and suppliers’ information

If you record information about other people, you are responsible for that information and for complying with the laws that apply to you, such as Moroccan Law No. 09-08. Because this information is stored only on your device, we do not have access to it.

14. Children

Fixor is a business management tool and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

15. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you (including Moroccan Law No. 09-08), you may have rights of access, rectification, opposition and deletion. Since the business information is only on your device, you can view, correct or delete it yourself in the App. For advertising data, you can use your Android settings and Google’s tools described above.

For any privacy question or request, contact us at support@vedoxe.com.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The current version, with its effective date and version number, is always available in the App (Settings → Legal) and on our website.
